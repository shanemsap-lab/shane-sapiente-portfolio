Reflections

My learning and growth throughout the BAOL program

Course Reflections

These reflections capture my learning journey and the ways each course has shaped my development as a leader.

OLCU 300: Organizational Behavior

This paper helped me understand how organizational culture and leadership styles can either empower or suppress employee voice. My experience at Apple mirrored many of these dynamics, reinforcing my belief in the importance of transformational leadership. It also deepened my appreciation for inclusive environments where feedback is valued and acted upon.

OLCU 350: Leadership & Professional Ethics

This experience shaped my ethical framework and reinforced the importance of empathy and integrity in leadership. It taught me how inner voice and values guide decisions even in emotionally charged environments. I learned that ethical leadership is not just about rules, but about honoring humanity and relationships.

OLCU 380: Research & Analytical Inquiry

This research deepened my understanding of how leadership and organizational structures affect equity. It reinforced my commitment to creating environments where all voices are heard and valued. I also reflected on how remote work policies can either support or hinder inclusion, depending on leadership's responsiveness.

OLCU 400: Theory & Practice of Leadership

This paper helped me connect theory to practice. Servant leadership resonates with my inner voice and values, while situational leadership reminds me that adaptability is key to empowering diverse teams. I've seen firsthand how leaders who listen and adjust their approach can unlock potential in others.

OLCU 425: Diverse & Multicultural Organizations

This project allowed me to apply inclusive leadership principles to a real-world organization. It affirmed my belief that leadership must be intentional, measurable, and aligned with values to truly foster belonging. I also reflected on how my own leadership role can influence equity and inclusion in tangible ways.

Program-Wide Reflection

The BAOL program has transformed my understanding of leadership. Through rigorous coursework and personal reflection, I've developed a framework for leading with authenticity, empathy, and purpose.

The most transformative insight has been recognizing the connection between inner voice and effective leadership. Each course built upon this foundation:

  • OLCU 300 showed me how organizations can silence or amplify voices
  • OLCU 350 taught me that ethics flows from values, not just rules
  • OLCU 380 gave me tools to research and understand these dynamics
  • OLCU 400 connected theory to my lived experience
  • OLCU 425 showed me how to build inclusive environments

Together, these courses have prepared me to lead in ways that honor both my values and the voices of those I serve.

Leadership Philosophy Statement

"Leadership is about listening to your inner voice and creating space for others to find and express theirs. Transformational leaders don't suppress voices—they amplify them."

My leadership philosophy has four pillars:

  • Authenticity: Leading from my values, even when it's difficult. My transition from Apple to Yale was an act of authentic leadership—choosing alignment over comfort.
  • Empathy: Seeking to understand before seeking to be understood. Every person has a story, and effective leadership requires hearing those stories.
  • Inclusion: Creating environments where every voice matters. This isn't passive—it requires intentional effort to amplify marginalized perspectives.
  • Growth: Embracing continuous learning. The BAOL program has shown me that leadership development is a lifelong journey, not a destination.

Future Goals

Looking ahead, I am committed to:

  • Applying transformational leadership in my role at Yale, creating environments where colleagues feel heard and valued
  • Continuing my education through professional development and potentially graduate studies in organizational development
  • Mentoring others who are navigating the tension between personal values and organizational culture
  • Contributing to research on employee voice, psychological safety, and values-aligned leadership

The BAOL program has given me the foundation. Now it's time to build upon it.
