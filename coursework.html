Course Artifacts

OLCU 300: Organizational Behavior

Leadership Motivation Organizational Culture Equity Theory

Paper: Apple Workers Unionizing

This paper analyzed how organizational culture and leadership styles can either empower or suppress employee voice. Using the Apple unionization movement as a case study, I explored the dynamics of employee motivation, equity theory, and the role of leadership in shaping workplace culture.

Competencies Demonstrated:

  • Analysis of organizational dynamics
  • Understanding of employee motivation theories
  • Critical evaluation of leadership's impact on culture
